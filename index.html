Your images stay with you.

Privacy first

Most online converters, and many AI tools, upload your files to someone else's server to process them. Magic Luna does all the work on your own computer: nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored and no one else sees your images.

  • 100% on your computer

    The conversion happens from start to finish on your machine. The app never sends your images, or anything else, over the internet.

  • No AI in the middle

    Your files are never sent to an AI service and are never used to train any model.

  • No account, no tracking

    No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Not in the app and not on this website.

  • Open source

    The full source code is public, so anyone can check exactly what Magic Luna does. See the code on GitHub (opens in a new window)

Lighter images, faster pages.

Performance

For anyone who designs or builds for the web, image weight is performance. Heavy PNGs slow down websites, hurt Core Web Vitals and use up your visitors' mobile data.

  • Lighter archives

    Storing thousands of images? According to Google, lossless WebP files are on average 26% smaller than PNGs. Results vary. Source: Google (opens in a new window)

  • Trusted for documentation

    Godot (opens in a new window), one of the most popular open source game engines, uses WebP for every image in its official documentation, with lossless compression whenever possible. Source: Godot guidelines (opens in a new window)

  • Same quality, pixel by pixel

    Magic Luna uses lossless compression, so the image looks exactly like the original. See Google's study (opens in a new window)

  • Transparency kept

    Transparent backgrounds stay transparent, just like in your PNG.

  • Works everywhere

    All modern browsers display WebP images.

Simple from start to finish.

How it works

  1. Select your PNG

    Click Select file, or press Ctrl+O, and choose an image on your computer.

  2. Convert to WebP

    Click Convert to WebP, or press Enter. It only takes a moment.

  3. Find it in the same folder

    Your WebP is saved right next to the original PNG, which stays untouched.

About the Windows security warning

Magic Luna is a small independent app and is not digitally signed yet. Because of this, Windows and your browser may show a warning the first time. This is expected and does not mean the app is harmful.

  • If your browser says the file “isn't commonly downloaded”: open your downloads list, click the three dots next to the file and choose Keep.
  • If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

Frequently asked questions

Is Magic Luna free?

Yes. It's free to download and use.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Download MagicLuna.exe and open it. Everything it needs is already inside the file.

Where do my images go?

Nowhere. Magic Luna reads the PNG and saves the WebP on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Is my original PNG changed or deleted?

No. Magic Luna creates a new WebP in the same folder and leaves the PNG exactly as it was. If a WebP with the same name is already there, it is replaced.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Version 1.0 runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Which formats can it convert?

Version 1.0 converts PNG images to WebP. More image formats are coming in future versions, always converted locally, right on your computer.
